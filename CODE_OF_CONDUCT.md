# Code of Conduct

SKAZ welcomes respectful participation in project issues, pull requests,
discussions and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; criticize the work, not the person.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experience levels and opinions.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep conversations relevant and respect other participants' time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual harassment,
deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information
without permission are not welcome.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions,
or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should reflect the
severity and context of the behavior; serious abuse may result in immediate removal.

There is currently no dedicated confidential conduct-reporting channel. Do not post
sensitive evidence or personal information in public threads. For content that
violates GitHub's policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting tools; those reports go
to GitHub, not to a private SKAZ inbox.

This is a project-specific policy, not an adoption of the Contributor Covenant.
Security vulnerabilities are covered separately in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
